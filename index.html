THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

A little name.
A growing idea.

Mita was the founder’s childhood nickname, a name spoken with affection. It carries a belief that sits at the heart of this business: with care and the right conditions, every Mita can grow into someone important, just as coffee grows into something valued.

Based in Addis Ababa, Mita Coffee is a female-owned developing startup bringing a professional coffee trading business together with a distinctive consumer identity.
